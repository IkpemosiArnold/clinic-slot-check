Checking

The type sets the length, the buffer after it, which doctors take it and whether a referral letter must be on file. A follow-up goes to the doctor the patient last saw. A number with no visit on record books a new-patient consult.

A slot is offered only if it starts on the quarter hour, sits inside a session of the right kind, clears every booking and its buffer, gives two hours' notice, avoids public holidays and falls inside the diary export. A doctor, a day or a type that cannot be read is asked about, not guessed.
